# IvyBridge Advisory — Website Design & Content Playbook
**Status:** approved baseline for AI-assisted website changes. **Updated:** 2026-10-20.

> **Read before editing:** [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), [AI_SITE_RULES.md](AI_SITE_RULES.md), [ADMISSIONS_DATA_GUIDE.md](ADMISSIONS_DATA_GUIDE.md), and this playbook. `AI_SITE_RULES.md` governs business rules and truthful public claims; this file governs their presentation and maintenance. If instructions conflict, **do not guess**: ask the owner.

## 1. Brand and audience
- Company: **IvyBridge Advisory / IvyBridge 領航留學**. Premium, transparent and personalized **U.S. master's admissions consulting** (not a PhD-only, STEM-only, visa-law or job-placement company).
- Primary audience: Asian/international undergraduate students at U.S. universities. Secondary: Taiwan-based undergraduate applicants. Welcome all majors and cross-disciplinary applicants.
- Core positioning: **高端、透明、一站式美國碩士申請顧問**. Career and Top 30 aspirations are strengths, not promised outcomes.
- Tone: clear, confident, warm, human and **specific**. No exaggerated claims, fear-based marketing, guaranteed outcomes, invented mentor qualifications or fake testimonials.
- Site-wide working languages: natural **Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)** and idiomatic English. Avoid mixed Simplified Chinese in public copy.

## 2. Visual style: what must remain standard
| Token / pattern | Rule |
|---|---|
| Primary color | Navy **#112E4A** and existing `ivy-800/900` tokens |
| Background | White **#FFFFFF**, restrained off-white **#F4F7FA** |
| Secondary type | Slate-gray / muted navy |
| Accent | Restrained antique gold **#C5A880** / **#D7B77D** |
| Headlines | Existing editorial serif / Georgia fallback; limited heading levels |
| Body | Existing system or NotoSansTC-friendly sans-serif |
| Panels | Light border, rounded corners (roughly 8–13 px), subtle shadow only where needed |
| Links & CTAs | Clear labels, focus indicators, consistent primary navy buttons; never fake clickable text |
| Spacing | Consistent vertical rhythm, readable paragraphs; avoid oversized empty space |
| Mobile | Single-column tools, compact cards, no horizontal scrolling except contained comparison tables |
| Accessibility | Semantic headings, accessible labels, real button/anchor elements, keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion |

**Do not** introduce neon/multi-color gradients, emoji-heavy cards, fake statistics, large arbitrary font-size shifts, trendy template visuals, enormous whitespace or a second competing navigation bar. Prefer adjusting `public/assets/fixes.css` rather than inserting duplicate CSS into all pages. Preserve existing logos, fonts, assets and established brand hierarchy.

## 4. Plan pricing and deliverables — DO NOT CHANGE
- **Essential 基礎申請指導: US$2,500**, 3 programs, +US$500 per additional program; profile and school advising, essays, **1** mock interview. **Student completes and submits online applications.**
- **Comprehensive 全程申請顧問: US$4,800**, 5 programs, +US$700 per additional program; profile and school advising, essays, **1** mock interview, advisor assistance with online application forms and submission preparation.
- **Elite 尊榮菁英全案: US$9,800**, up to **12 U.S. programs**, +US$1,000 for each U.S. program **beyond 12**, **3** mock interviews; application-form and general visa documentation assistance. **Non-U.S. applications quoted separately** and not counted in the 12.
- Each university/program combination counts separately, including two programs in the same university. Third-party fees are separate. Students must personally verify and perform mandatory declarations, verification or payments.
- Homepage plan cards do **not** show prices; the service-detail page does. Modular assistance stays a secondary quote-based option.
- Source-of-truth plan content: `data/plans.json` and `scripts/render-plans.py`. Review generated diffs. **Do not hand-edit generated plan blocks and then run the generator without comparing changes.**
- Never invent essay revision limits, callback SLAs, adviser-student ratios, scholarship outcomes, visa eligibility or admissions guarantees.

## 5. Editorial content by page
- **Home:** promise and audience, differentiated value, three service paths, verifiable trust, one primary consultation CTA. Do not clutter with duplicate plan taxonomies or unverified acceptance feeds.
- **Services:** the three package details, comparison table, four-step process, short FAQ, modular help, honest handoff/responsibility policy. Keep pricing and any extra-program rules in sync with the approved JSON.
- **Study in the USA:** thoughtful academic/career advantages; degree and program fit; CPT/OPT/STEM OPT cautions; international support; **departure and post-offer checklist**. Avoid legal interpretations or automatic work rights.
- **Unified Study Guide:** four stages — **(1) understand master’s study, (2) programs and careers, (3) budget and costs, (4) application preparation**. Tools start collapsed; deeper reading follows, then stories/consultation. This owner-approved sequence supersedes the earlier Explorer order. No duplicate free-text university search bar or company dropdown. Do not invent salaries.
- **Cases:** show only approved/consented outcomes; illustrative scenarios must be explicitly labeled in cards and detail modals.
- **About:** only verifiable founder / adviser experience and documented processes.
- **Contact:** current forms prepare or download data locally; **they do not submit, send, schedule or promise a callback** until a real integration is approved.
- **Blog:** unpublished article teasers stay non-clickable and honestly labeled. Do not turn placeholder previews into fake published articles.

## 6. Graduate admissions data: strict source contract
- **Only edit:** `public/assets/admissions-data.js`. UI is `public/assets/school-explorer.js`. How-to and missing school/field inventory: [ADMISSIONS_DATA_GUIDE.md](ADMISSIONS_DATA_GUIDE.md).
- Curated list of **50 U.S. universities is not an official ranking**; a university can have separate `stem` and `business` entries. No generic “this school's master's acceptance rate.”
- A record must provide: numerical percentage, **exact master's program name**, admission year, direct supporting URL, source quality (`official`, `official-calculated`, or `secondary`) and limitations. Prefer official school figures, then careful third-party MBA reports.
- Do not confuse **MBA** with MSBA, MFin or other business degrees. Do not use undergraduate admits, aggregate graduate admits, or enrollments divided by applications as a master’s program rate. If no trustworthy source exists, keep `null`; never generate plausible-looking data.
- Show publication year and clear source. Secondary numbers must be labeled secondary. Recheck sources periodically, and confirm any manual addition does not break the 50-school dropdown or language switching.
- The **IvyBridge 85%** is an **unverified draft statistic** until the brand supplies student records, numerator, denominator, cohort years and precise definition. It must remain explicitly “待核實 / Unverified”; **remove before public launch if unsupported**. Never position it as directly comparable to a school's program admission rate.

## 7. Interaction QA requirements
When changing navigation or tools, verify **all 10 Chinese + 10 English routes** and:
1. No broken internal HTML links, fragments, target IDs or duplicate IDs.
2. One consistent header/footer and one global chapter sidebar per page; sidebar anchors scroll to real sections.
3. In-page link, browser back/forward, Chinese↔English switch (with `?plan=elite`, `#essential`, and Explorer anchors).
4. 50-school dropdown, change STEM↔Business, official/secondary/null state, correct source/year; no empty dropdown.
5. Budget controls update amounts; timeline accordion opens/closes with keyboard; career field/region updates role and illustrative employers.
6. Mobile width (375 px), tablet width (~768 px), desktop width (~1440 px), overflow, menu, sticky TOC, focus indicators.
7. Forms validate and **only download or copy** pending explicit backend integration.
8. Keep `noindex` on unapproved preview content. Do not claim Vercel or live browser testing that was not performed.

## 8. GitHub / Vercel handoff contract
- Review the **current active PR branch** and incoming work before edits; never blindly overwrite accepted changes.
- Commit only to a **feature branch**; open/update a **Pull Request**; Vercel Preview for owner QA.
- **NEVER merge to main until the owner explicitly approves the PR.** Do not reset/force-push unrelated commits.
- Before handoff: summarize each file changed, tests passed/failed, missing datasets, nonworking external integrations and preview status.
- Keep diffs small, reversible and clearly titled. Any unrequested feature or fee change should be discussed first.

## 9. Release blockers (not cleared by visual QA alone)
- Verified IvyBridge success statistics, advisor biographies, approved cases and testimonials.
- Valid contact channel, working form transport and privacy notice; any contractual/return/refund wording.
- Verified legal/visa guidance with authoritative links; remove unsupported performance claims.
- Verify live mobile/browser interactions, bilingual parity and proper indexing only when ready to publish.

**Future AI instruction:** If the owner hasn't approved changing a standard, keep it. If a fact cannot be verified, show a clearly labeled unknown rather than fabricate an attractive statistic.
