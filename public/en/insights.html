Study Abroad: From Program Research to Departure

Program and admissions guide · Visa and daily life

After Admission: A Preparation Checklist

  • Compare total costs, curriculum and offer conditions; confirm acceptance and deposit deadlines.
  • Follow school instructions for enrollment documents and financial evidence; check names against your passport.
  • Check the student visa process through official channels. Complete your own attestations and interview.
  • Review housing terms, insurance, transport and arrival costs, and keep emergency contacts accessible.

EducationUSA — Your 5 Steps to U.S. Study

A Master’s Application Timeline

Adapt this outline to each program’s deadlines and your own readiness.

12–18 months before entry

Research programs and prerequisites; assess your budget, academic background and testing requirements.

6–12 months before entry

Refine your program list, contact recommenders and develop application essays.

Before each deadline

Review forms and documents, confirm recommender submissions and submit according to the school’s instructions.

After admission

Compare offers, complete enrollment requirements, check official visa instructions and plan accommodation.
