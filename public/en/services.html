Responsibilities and fees

  • In Essential, applicants manage forms and submission. In Comprehensive and Elite, advisors complete application information and coordinate submission to minimize administrative work.
  • Applicants provide accurate information and approve final materials. Personal attestations, verification, payment and interviews follow the institution’s requirements and may require the applicant’s own action.
  • All fees are in USD. Each program counts separately, including different programs at the same university. School application fees, visa fees, tests, translations and other third-party charges are excluded.
  • Visa form and document assistance is included in Elite; decisions rest with the relevant authorities. Essays reflect the applicant’s own experience. Scope, timelines and revision arrangements are confirmed in the agreement; admission is not guaranteed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What counts as one program?

Each individual degree/application program counts separately, even when two programs belong to the same university.

Do I still need to complete forms myself?

Comprehensive and Elite include hands-on form support. Applicants must provide accurate information, review materials and personally complete any attestations, payment or identity verification required by institutions.

Will my essays be customized?

Guidance is tailored to program prompts and your authentic background. Revision rounds, deliverables and deadlines are confirmed before signing.

Are application or visa fees included?

No. Third-party charges such as application, test, translation and visa fees are separate.

Can I book an individual service?

Yes. Request school selection, essay editing, CV feedback or interviews independently. Explore custom services →
