    04

    Application preparation

    Work back from each program’s deadline to plan documents, interviews and departure.

    • Confirm testing, essays, recommenders and document requirements.
    • Review complete submissions and prepare for each program’s interview process.
    • After admission, confirm enrollment, visa steps, housing and insurance.

    A Master’s Application Timeline

    Adapt this outline to each program’s deadlines and your own readiness.

    12–18 months before entry

    Research programs and prerequisites; assess your budget, academic background and testing requirements.

    6–12 months before entry

    Refine your program list, contact recommenders and develop application essays.

    Before each deadline

    Review forms and documents, confirm recommender submissions and submit according to the school’s instructions.

    After admission

    Compare offers, complete enrollment requirements, check official visa instructions and plan accommodation.

    PRACTICAL TRAINING

    CPT, OPT and STEM OPT: know the requirements first

    CPT · Curricular Practical Training

    Employment authorization depends on curricular integration, DSO approval, F-1 status and university policies. Not every student is automatically eligible.

    OPT · Optional Practical Training

    OPT has filing windows and eligibility requirements. A STEM OPT extension depends on qualified degrees and employers, along with other DHS rules.

    Educational information only, not immigration legal advice. Confirm individual eligibility with your school's DSO, USCIS or qualified counsel.

    AFTER ADMISSION

    Moving abroad: from offer to arrival

    Your pre-departure checklist for decisions and practical preparation.

    • Compare the total program cost, degree requirements and offer conditions before deposit deadlines.
    • Prepare university-requested financial and identity documents, confirming all passport details.
    • Follow official I-20, SEVIS and visa processes and complete personal attestations and interviews yourself.
    • Plan housing, insurance, transportation, living expenses and emergency contacts.
    • Review arrival deadlines, orientation and international student office resources.
    • Keep secure copies of essential documents and allow for unexpected processing time.
