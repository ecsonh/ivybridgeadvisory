Why Study in the USA?

Start with your academic and career goals to decide whether a U.S. master’s degree is the right next step.

Academic quality · Internships and OPT · Flexible study and interdisciplinary pathways · International student support and daily life

Academic quality: look beyond rankings

Compare core courses, faculty expertise, capstone or thesis requirements, cohort size and accreditation. Evaluate academic, financial and personal fit alongside reputation.

Internships and OPT: check eligibility before planning

Review career services, internship support and course schedules. CPT, OPT and STEM OPT have separate eligibility requirements. Admission does not automatically provide work authorization; confirm current requirements with the school’s DSO and relevant authorities.

Flexible study and interdisciplinary pathways

Check required mathematics, programming or subject prerequisites. Confirm whether programs accept other academic backgrounds, allow electives across departments and require extra coursework that affects cost or time to graduate.

International student support and daily life

Review international student advising, housing, insurance, transport, wellbeing support and alumni communities. Include location and living costs in your program comparison before applying.
